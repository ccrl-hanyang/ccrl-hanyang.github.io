Research

What we work on

Error-correcting codes sit at the centre. Cryptography, learning, and radar are where the same algebra keeps turning up.

ECC

Error-correcting codes

Codes are the reason a message survives a channel that corrupts it. We design them, and we also work the inverse problem: recovering the code itself when nobody told us which one was used.

  • Error-correcting schemes for wireless communication systems
  • Error-correcting schemes for memory, including NAND flash and DRAM
  • Blind detection and identification of coding schemes in non-cooperative settings
PQC

Post-quantum cryptography

Lattices and codes both give hardness assumptions no quantum algorithm is known to break. We build on them, and we attack implementations to find where the theory leaks.

  • Lattice-based post-quantum cryptography
  • Code-based post-quantum cryptography
  • Fully homomorphic encryption for deep learning
  • Side-channel attacks and defences for post-quantum algorithms
ML / DL

Machine and deep learning

Learning systems fail in ways classical estimators do not. Coding theory turns out to be a useful lens on why, and on how to make them fail less often.

  • Learning-based methods for communication systems and autonomous vehicles
  • Robustness of deep networks via feature disentanglement and error-correcting output codes
  • Understanding how learning and inference behave across model families
Radar

Radar signal processing

Detection under clutter is a hypothesis test with a moving threshold. We derive the optimal parameters, then ask what a network can learn that the derivation cannot express.

  • Analysis of CFAR algorithms and derivation of optimal parameters
  • Deep-learning CFAR for military and commercial radar
  • Camera–radar fusion with multi-modal networks for autonomous driving
